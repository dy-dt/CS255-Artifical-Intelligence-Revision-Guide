Rationality

Rationality (for the purpose of this course) says we can derive a conclusion from a particular set of premises. We define rational behaviour as "doing the right thing", that is we do the thing that maximises goal achievement given the current information (capitalism says high).

But human???

You may have noticed that humans don't exactly behave rationally (for better or for worse)
